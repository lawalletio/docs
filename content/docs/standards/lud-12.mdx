---
title: LUD-12 · Comments
description: Attach a short message to a payment.
---

The pay response advertises `commentAllowed` (max length). The payer adds `&comment=...` to the callback.

**In LaWallet:** the platform accepts comments on every address, and the wallets show a comment field when it's allowed.

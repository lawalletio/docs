`alice@domain.com` is a pointer to an [LUD-06](/docs/standards/lud-06) pay endpoint at `https://domain.com/.well-known/lnurlp/alice`.

<Flow steps={[['Payer', 'GET /.well-known/lnurlp/alice'], ['Platform', 'payRequest'], ['Payer', 'callback?amount='], ['Platform', 'BOLT11'], ['Payer']]} />

**In LaWallet:** every user gets an address on your domain. The platform serves it, and can forward it (alias/redirect) to another address.

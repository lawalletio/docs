---
title: NIP-07 · Browser Extension
description: Sign in with a Nostr browser extension. Your key never leaves it.
---

The extension injects `window.nostr`. The site calls `getPublicKey()` and `signEvent()`, and the extension asks you to approve.

**In LaWallet:** web login for the platform and admin dashboard. On Android the wallet uses the same idea with an external signer app ([NIP-55](https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips/blob/master/55.md), e.g. Amber).

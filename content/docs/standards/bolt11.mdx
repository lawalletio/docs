---
title: BOLT11 · Invoice
description: The regular single-use Lightning invoice.
---

`lnbc…`: amount, payment hash, description, expiry, signed by the receiver. One invoice, one payment.

Over QR or NFC it travels as the URI `lightning:lnbc…`.

**In LaWallet:** every receive flow ends in a BOLT11 invoice, and the POS shows it as QR and as an [NFC tag](/docs/standards/android-hce).
